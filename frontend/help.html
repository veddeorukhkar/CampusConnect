Help Center

Find quick answers on using CampusConnect. Still stuck? Reach out to your campus admin office.

Getting Started

Create an account from the Register page using your student ID and college email. Once registered, log in to access your personal dashboard.

Submitting a Complaint

Go to the Complaints page, click "New Complaint", fill in the details, and submit. Track its status anytime.

Service Requests

Use the Service Requests page to ask for ID cards, transcripts, certificates and more.

Registering for Events

Open the Events page, click "Register", fill your contact details, and confirm. It'll appear under "My Registered Events".

Still need help?

Check our FAQ page or contact your campus administration office.
