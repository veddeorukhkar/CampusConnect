Frequently Asked Questions

Who can create a CampusConnect account?

Any enrolled student with a valid student ID and college email can register. Admin accounts are created separately.

How long does it take for a complaint to be resolved?

Resolution time depends on the category and priority. Track live updates from the Complaints page.

Can I edit a complaint after submitting it?

Complaints can't be edited once submitted. Submit a new one referencing the earlier complaint if needed.

How do I know my event registration was successful?

You'll get a success message immediately, and it'll show under "My Registered Events" on the Events page.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Use "Forgot password?" on the Login page to receive reset instructions.

Is my personal data safe?

Yes. Passwords are encrypted and access is protected. See our Privacy Policy.
