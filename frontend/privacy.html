Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect your registration details (name, student ID, email, department, year) and content you submit (complaints, requests, event registrations).

2. How We Use Your Information

Used solely to operate the platform — authentication, processing requests, and showing relevant notices/events.

3. Data Security

Passwords are encrypted using bcrypt and never stored in plain text. Admin access is role-restricted.

4. Data Sharing

Your data is only visible to college administrators for resolving complaints/requests. Never sold to third parties.

5. Your Rights

Update your profile anytime from the Profile page. For deletion requests, contact your campus administration.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact your campus administration office.
